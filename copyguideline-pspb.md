## **System Definition**

The **PSPB Landing Page** is an official public-facing page promoting the **Partisipasi Semesta Pendidikan Bermutu** program, launched by the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education (Kemendikdasmen) under **Permendikdasmen No. 3 of 2026**.

The program aims to improve the equitable distribution of education quality in Indonesia by facilitating collaboration between the government, private sector, and civil society in channeling infrastructure support transparently through an integrated information system.

This page serves two primary objectives: **educating users about priority programs** and **driving the desire to contribute**. Copy MUST reflect: process transparency, measurable real-world impact, ease of program selection, and equal-footing collaboration — not one-sided philanthropy.

---

## **Primary Users**

* **Corporate Partners** — companies looking to channel contributions as part of a social responsibility or strategic partnership program  
* **Individual Partners** — individuals who want to contribute to national education priority programs  
* **Organizational Partners** — foundations, NGOs, or non-governmental institutions looking to collaborate

Users are assumed to:

* Not necessarily be familiar with the government's education ecosystem  
* Be motivated by tangible impact, transparency, and ease of process  
* Need assurance that their contribution will reach the right recipients and be well-managed

DO NOT assume users already know what priority programs are available. DO NOT assume users are familiar with government education terminology. The benefits of contributing must always be explained concretely.

---

## **Core User Intent**

Users come to this page to:

* Understand what PSPB is and why it matters  
* Learn what priority programs are available and can be supported  
* Understand how the contribution process works  
* Feel confident that their contribution is transparent and accurately targeted  
* Take their first step toward contributing

Copy MUST prioritize:

* Clarity of contribution benefits (not just system feature descriptions)  
* Ease of understanding the process flow  
* Trust through transparency and verified data  
* An equal-footing, persuasive invitation — not bureaucratic or pleading in tone

## **Content Behavior Rules (PSPB Landing Page–Specific)**

* DO NOT promise specific impact outcomes or timelines that have not been verified.  
* DO NOT imply that a contribution will be approved without a discussion process with Kemendikdasmen.  
* Always frame contributions as two-way collaboration — not a one-way transaction.  
* Avoid bureaucratic or overly legalistic tone.  
* Avoid pleading or self-deprecating tone — position Partners as equals who create real impact.  
* Always distinguish between:  
  * Data availability vs. confirmed disbursement of support  
  * Submitting a commitment form vs. completing the full collaboration process

Examples:

✅ "Choose a location and program to support based on verified school needs data."  
 ❌ "Your support will be received directly by schools in need."

✅ "We will guide you through the disbursement and reporting process."  
 ❌ "Sign up now and your support will be delivered immediately."

### **Value Framing (Critical Rule)**

Copy MUST reference at least one of PSPB's three core values in each major section:

* **Accurately targeted** — needs data verified by Local Government and connected to Dapodik  
* **Easy process** — Partners simply select a program and submit a commitment form; the system guides the rest  
* **Transparent progress** — Partners can monitor the development and impact of their contribution

---

## **Empty States**

Empty states MUST:

* Clearly explain why content is not yet available  
* Provide a concrete next step for Partners  
* Avoid implying a system error unless confirmed

Example:  
 "Needs data for this area is currently being verified by the Local Government. Try selecting a different area or check back later."

---

## **Error & Validation Messaging**

* Errors MUST specify whether the issue is:  
  * Data-related (incomplete form)  
  * System-related (technical issue)  
  * Process-related (stage not yet available)  
* Where possible, direct users to a corrective action without referencing internal system logic.

Example:  
 "Complete all required fields before submitting the collaboration commitment form."
